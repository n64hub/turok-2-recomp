# Turok 2's soak plan: one round through the game's scenes for the desktop soak, a play of its Linux build by this plan
# for 205 s that fails on a crash, the program exiting or the picture standing still for 10 s. Each line is a step at
# seconds since the program started: key <name> [ms held], down and up <name>, shot <name>, peek <addr>, until <addr>
# <mask> <value> <timeout s> [key [ms]] (waits until the game's 32-bit word at addr, anded with mask, is value, pressing
# key every 1.5 s meanwhile; the later steps move on by the wait, and a wait unmet fails the play), and random <key>,...
# (one of them every 0.3-1.2 s) until random off. The keys are src/game/config.cpp's (Enter Start, E A, Q B, Tab L, W A
# S D the C buttons, the arrows the stick, Space R).
#
# A round: the boot, Start (Enter) through the title into the main menu (mode 8), Single and New Game, past the
# Controller Pak note and the Important screen, into the first cinematic (mode 11, Adon and the Eye of Truth), and the
# Port of Adia's opening cinematic (12), which loads the first level. Start skips neither: the two take about 2 minutes
# 15 s, most of the round, which is why it runs to 3 minutes 25 s with the boot. Then, with the player in control (13),
# 12 s of random moves, turns, jumps and weapon changes, the map (Tab) open and closed, the pause menu (Enter) with
# Mission Status (closed with Q, B) and Inventory (closed with Enter, its OK) opened, Q back to the game, and random
# input to the end. The word at 0x8011AC88 is the game's mode (tools/survey.js says what each is); each `until` waits on
# it, so the round keeps step with a slow boot. The Port of Adia's first level change is minutes of play away, past the
# round: the level the cinematics load is this round's one.
