**What's new:** mouse look is on from the first start (General, Mouse Sensitivity, 50%; 0 turns it off and leaves the cursor free). If you played v0.1.0 or v0.1.1, your saved settings have it at 0, so turn it up there. In widescreen, opening the map (Tab) no longer draws a thin line down the picture at each of the old 4:3 edges. Up looks up: moving the mouse up, the up arrow and the right stick pushed up now look up; in v0.1.x they looked down, since the game looks down with its stick pushed up, as a plane's stick does.
