- **Draw Distance** (Graphics): Far, the new default, draws all of the level the game has loaded. The console leaves out whole buildings and walls its fog would hide, and with Fog off they showed as sky, coming and going as the camera turned. With Fog off, Far also draws the world four times as far, which Fog Off did on its own before. Normal draws what the console does.
- **Nothing near you goes missing in busy views:** the game keeps a short list each frame of what it is about to draw, and where the list filled up (in the Port of Adia's towns, most of all at Far) it left out what came last, the ground and walls nearest the camera. The list now has eight times the room.
