An update to Turok 2: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: the weapon in Turok's hands keeps the game's own look at any Field of View.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**What's new:**

- **Field of View:** the weapon in Turok's hands is drawn with the game's own view whatever the Graphics tab's Field of View, so it no longer shrinks and floats away from the corner of the screen at a wider view, or grows and leaves the screen at a narrower one. The world still widens or narrows as before. Checked with the Tek Bow in the Port of Adia at 70%, 100%, 115% and 150%.

**Windows:** unzip `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.23-windows-x64.zip` and run `Turok2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.23-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Turok2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `fb0400f21e3f043939ab56500c7b12a3231006f1`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** weapons other than the Talon and the Tek Bow, the levels after the Port of Adia, a whole playthrough (reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-2-recomp/issues)), and the Linux build on a GPU other than NVIDIA's.

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/turok-2](https://n64hub.com/turok-2/).
