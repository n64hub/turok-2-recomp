An update to Turok 2: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: a Movement Speed setting, a Field of View setting, and 8X anti-aliasing.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**What's new:**

- **Movement Speed** (General, 100%): how fast you walk, strafe and back up, up to 200%. It scales the same speed the game's own hidden debug setting does; at 200% you cover twice the ground in the same time.
- **Field of View** (Graphics, 100%): widens the game's own view up to 150%, or narrows it down to 70%. The game sets its view per area of a level, and this scales whatever that is; cinematics keep the game's own view.
- **MS Anti-Aliasing** goes up to **8X**, where the GPU can take it (greyed out where it can't). 2X stays the default.

**Windows:** unzip `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.13-windows-x64.zip` and run `Turok2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.13-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Turok2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `fb0400f21e3f043939ab56500c7b12a3231006f1`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** Movement Speed and Field of View past the Port of Adia, and a whole playthrough (reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-2-recomp/issues)), long play sessions, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only).

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/turok-2](https://n64hub.com/turok-2/) (Field of View and Movement Speed there too; anti-aliasing isn't available in a browser).
