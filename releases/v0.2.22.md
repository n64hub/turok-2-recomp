An update to Turok 2: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: the weapon wheel picks the weapon the stick points at.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**What's new:**

- **Weapon wheel:** pushing the stick (or the arrow keys) towards a weapon on the wheel picks that weapon. Up and down were swapped there: the stick is turned over for looking, so that pushing it up looks up, and the wheel read it turned over too. Looking is as before.

**Windows:** unzip `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.22-windows-x64.zip` and run `Turok2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.22-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Turok2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `fb0400f21e3f043939ab56500c7b12a3231006f1`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** the change on Windows (the wheel was played on the Linux build), the levels after the Port of Adia, a whole playthrough (reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-2-recomp/issues)), and the Linux build on a GPU other than NVIDIA's.

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/turok-2](https://n64hub.com/turok-2/).
