An update to Turok 2: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: the stick and the keys reach the picture in about half the time.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**What's new:**

- **Much less input lag:** a turn or a look used to reach the picture two of the game's frames after the game read it, as the game's own order put the camera's move after the frame that drew the world, and each frame then waited most of its time with that read already old. The game now moves the camera before it draws, starts each frame just in time for its turn on the screen, and shows it as soon as it is drawn. Measured from a press of the stick or a key to the picture changing, the median is about 100 ms instead of 200 ms (of which about 70 ms is the measurement's own capture of the window). The map and every other button answer as before.

**Windows:** unzip `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.16-windows-x64.zip` and run `Turok2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.16-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Turok2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `fb0400f21e3f043939ab56500c7b12a3231006f1`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Known:** the cutscenes, which run at 15 frames a second, judder a little more than before, and in the opening cutscene at the Port of Adia a limb close to the camera can flash for a frame.

**Not checked yet:** the change on Windows (it was measured on the Linux build), a whole playthrough (reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-2-recomp/issues)), long play sessions, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only).

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/turok-2](https://n64hub.com/turok-2/), with the same change.
