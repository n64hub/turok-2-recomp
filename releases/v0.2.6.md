An update to Turok 2: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: multiplayer's split screen fills a widescreen window, each player's view with its own HUD.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**What's new:**

- Split screen in a widescreen window (Aspect Ratio Expand), with two, three or four players: every view is widened with its own HUD. The lower views had no HUD at all, and the top half of two players was stretched across the window; each view's counters now sit in its own corners, as HUD Placement puts them, and its crosshair at its centre. A thin black strip down the window's right edge is gone too.

**Windows:** unzip `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.6-windows-x64.zip` and run `Turok2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.6-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Turok2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `fb0400f21e3f043939ab56500c7b12a3231006f1`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** the levels after the first and a whole playthrough (reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-2-recomp/issues)), long play sessions, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). In three and four players in a widescreen window, the weapon in the right-hand views still sits where it would at 4:3.

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/turok-2](https://n64hub.com/turok-2/).
