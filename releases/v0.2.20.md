An update to Turok 2: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: the quicker controls of v0.2.16 are back, and the map still doesn't stutter.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**What's new:**

- **The quicker controls are back:** v0.2.18's map fix was meant for the frames drawn with the map open, but v0.2.18 and v0.2.19 paced every frame that way, as v0.2.15 did: a stick or key press took one retrace (17 ms) longer to reach the picture everywhere, and the browser version presented fewer frames. Only frames with the map open are paced that way now, and the map still doesn't flicker or stutter. While the map is open, a press takes one retrace longer to reach the picture than with it closed.

**Windows:** unzip `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.20-windows-x64.zip` and run `Turok2Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Turok2Recompiled-v0.2.20-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Turok2Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `fb0400f21e3f043939ab56500c7b12a3231006f1`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** the change on Windows (it was checked on the Linux build, in the Port of Adia with the map open and closed, at 120 Hz), a whole playthrough (reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-2-recomp/issues)), and the Linux build on a GPU other than NVIDIA's.

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/turok-2](https://n64hub.com/turok-2/).
